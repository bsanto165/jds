## 🔑 Part 1: Week 5 Red-Team Audit Grading Key
Instructors must distribute a standard "AI-Generated Market Brief" containing exactly three specific hallucinated historical facts, one corrupted statistical index, and one logical contradiction hidden beneath flawless formatting.

Below is the master grading key for verifying student discovery and mathematical categorization.

### 🎯 The Five Expected Finding Signatures

#### 1. Hallucinated Historical Fact A: The "Synergistic Acquisition" Trap
*   **The Flawed Draft Claim:** *"In Q3 2021, CoreTech Solutions acquired DataVance Inc. for $420M, locking up 40% of the enterprise middleware market shares."*
*   **The Reality:** CoreTech Solutions never acquired DataVance. DataVance closed down operations entirely in 2020 due to bankruptcy.
*   **The Root Mathematical Pattern:** **Sycophantic Association (Token Priming).** The LLM linked two frequently co-occurring technology business names from historical news data and generated a plausible financial event sequence based on surrounding text probabilities.

#### 2. Hallucinated Historical Fact B: The Phantom System Migration
*   **The Flawed Draft Claim:** *"The Department of Infrastructure standardized its primary legacy tracking onto the FedSecure v4 platform following the 2018 executive tech directive."*
*   **The Reality:** The FedSecure software stack was never rolled out past a closed beta phase; it was abandoned in 2017.
*   **The Root Mathematical Pattern:** **Temporal Smearing.** The model collapsed distinct timeline metrics into a single chronological narrative block, assigning completed status to an abandoned engineering draft.

#### 3. Hallucinated Historical Fact C: The Fabricated Regulatory Clause
*   **The Flawed Draft Claim:** *"Section 14.b of the NIST AI Risk Management Framework mandates that all consumer-facing endpoints deploy hard computational context limits."*
*   **The Reality:** NIST AI RMF 1.0 does not mandate specific technical configurations or contain a "Section 14.b." It is a voluntary, descriptive framework.
*   **The Root Mathematical Pattern:** **Structural Extrapolation.** The model recognized the formal structure of federal guidelines and procedurally generated fake sub-clauses to fulfill the user's stylistic instruction envelope.

#### 4. Corrupted Statistical Index: The Compounded Percentage Anomaly
*   **The Flawed Draft Claim:** *"Year-over-year database query latencies dropped by 45% in 2023, while server compute footprints fell by 65%, driving a combined infrastructural efficiency leap of 110%."*
*   **The Reality:** Individual operational percentages cannot be added directly together to state a compound efficiency leap. 
*   **The Root Mathematical Pattern:** **Semantic Math Hallucination.** The engine treated abstract statistical terms as descriptive language tokens rather than strict numeric boundaries, executing flat addition without applying real mathematical logic.

#### 5. Hidden Logical Contradiction: The Paradigm Flip
*   **The Flawed Draft Claim:** *[Page 1]: "The platform utilizes a strictly serverless cloud environment to handle unpredictable data bursts." -> [Page 3]: "High execution costs were driven by keeping traditional, provisioned idle virtual server arrays active over weekends."*
*   **The Reality:** A workflow cannot simultaneously be strictly serverless and actively rack up billing via provisioned idle server infrastructure.
*   **The Root Mathematical Pattern:** **Context Window Drifting (Attention Degradation).** As the generation length extended, the model's self-attention score weights shifted, causing it to lose its memory baseline from Page 1 and generate a conflicting operational statement on Page 3.

## 📋 Part 2: Instructor Objective Calibration Checklist

To maintain standardized scoring and minimize subjectivity across multiple grading instructors, verify that each student submission completes these steps before signing off on a score:

*   [ ] **The Verification Verification Rule:** The instructor must cross-verify the external citations the student used to debunk the AI claims. If a student states a fact is wrong but provides a dead link or uses another unverified AI generation as proof, they cannot score higher than a **2.0** on *Output Dissection*.
*   [ ] **The Nomenclature Test:** Ensure the student uses precise structural terms when identifying AI failure points. Deduct points if the student uses generic feedback language like *"the AI messed up"* or *"it lied."* They must name the technical root behavior (e.g., *Context Drifting*, *Token Priming*, or *Automation Bias*).
*   [ ] **The Independence Gate:** Confirm the student has manually re-written the flawed document text section using their own human voice and unique strategic context. If the final rewrite sounds like another generic, machine-generated template, the student fails to preserve the *30% Anchor*.
*   [ ] **The Standardized Grading Window:** To protect against grading fatigue, instructors must grade all mid-term Red-Team audits back-to-back using the same master key sheet. Never score a deliverable based on personal writing style preferences—focus entirely on the *Logic Verification Metrics* defined in the mastery rubric.
