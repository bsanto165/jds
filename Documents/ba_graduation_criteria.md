# Business Analyst Upskilling Program: Final Graduation & Role Transition Criteria
**Employee Name:** [Insert Employee Name]  
**Current Role:** Admin Help Desk Tier 1 Support  
**Target Role:** Junior Business Analyst  
**Program Duration:** 6 Months (24 Weeks)  

---

## 📋 1. Executive Program Overview
This document outlines the strict qualification metrics and final evaluation criteria required to transition the employee from an **Admin Help Desk Tier 1 Support** role to a **Junior Business Analyst** headcount. 

To achieve full graduation status, the employee must prove mastery across the four foundational pillars via milestone badge verification, real-world project artifact reviews, and consistent execution during the two-month AI-assisted active implementation phase.

---

## 🎖️ 2. Milestone Badge Verification (Months 1–4)
Prior to final review, Human Resources and the Direct Manager must verify the successful acquisition of all four foundational competency badges. Each badge requires a minimum scoring average of **3.5 out of 5.0** based on the weekly grading rubrics.

### Month 1: The Foundations Badge
* **Focus:** Transitioning from reactive ticket handling to active listening and root-cause analysis.
* **Core Artifact Required:** 1-Page Meeting Summary & 30-Day Support Queue Trend Analysis.

### Month 2: The Technical Analytics Badge
* **Focus:** Transitioning from viewing desktop applications to querying data structures and building visual dashboards.
* **Core Artifact Required:** Verified SQL Query Log & 1-Page Executive Power BI/Tableau Dashboard.

### Month 3: The Business Case Architecture Badge
* **Focus:** Moving past localized technical complaints to translate workflows into corporate financial metrics and strategic proposals.
* **Core Artifact Required:** Mini-Business Case Cost-Benefit Template & 3-Slide Executive Pitch Deck.

### Month 4: The Elite Liaison Badge
* **Focus:** Mastering requirement gathering interviews, managing scope creep, and aligning conflicting stakeholder priorities.
* **Core Artifact Required:** Documented Functional Requirements Sheet & Scope Creep Response Script.

---

## 🤖 3. AI-Assisted Implementation Metrics (Months 5–6)
During the final two months, the employee partnered with an AI Operations Assistant to manage daily workspace workflows and implement real, reasonable changes to the environment. Growth is determined via objective daily performance telemetry aggregated by the AI.

* **Minimum Proficiency Threshold:** The employee must achieve a cumulative 60-day rolling average score of **7.5 or higher** on a 10.0 scale.
* **Weight Distribution:**
  * **System Efficiency (40%):** Quantitative evidence of automated tasks or optimized workflows.
  * **Operational Velocity (30%):** Acceleration of daily support operations against the pre-program baseline.
  * **Strategic Quality (30%):** Adherence to strict business analyst documentation and implementation guardrails.

---

## 🎤 4. Final Capstone Role Simulation & Presentation
The ultimate gatekeeper for graduation is a live **15-Minute Capstone Presentation** delivered directly to the Management Team and an HR representative. 

### Presentation Requirements
The employee must choose a real, recurring bottleneck from their actual Tier 1 Help Desk workspace and present an end-to-end business analysis case showing:
1. **The Data Evidence:** The visual dashboard proving the frequency and operational impact of the bottleneck.
2. **The Root Cause:** A fully fleshed-out "5 Whys" or Fishbone Diagram demonstrating systemic failure points rather than user error.
3. **The Financial Justification:** A clear Cost-Benefit Analysis proving how much labor or licensing money the company saves by implementing their fix.
4. **The Implementation Strategy:** A process diagram illustrating how data and personnel workflows change under the optimized design.

### Evaluation Criteria
The live panel will score the presentation using a binary **Pass/Fail standard** across three capabilities:
* **Translation Ability:** Can the employee present technical issues using plain, high-level business logic?
* **Poise under Pushback:** Can the employee handle a stakeholder introducing aggressive scope creep or challenging their ROI metrics during Q&A?
* **Artifact Execution:** Are the technical process maps, SQL-driven assertions, and financial calculations flawless and fully audible?
