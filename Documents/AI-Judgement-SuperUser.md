## 🛠️ Part 2: Practical Homework Assignments & Case Studies

### 📝 Assignment 1: The AI Overconfidence Audit (Module 3 / Lesson 3.1)
*   **Objective:** Develop the critical discernment required to pressure-test polished, highly confident AI outputs.
*   **The Task:** The instructor provides students with an AI-generated 3-page market research brief that contains exactly three subtly hallucinated historical facts, a false statistical index, and a logical contradiction wrapped in flawless formatting.
*   **Deliverable:** A formal Red-Team Verification Audit Report. The student must isolate every factual claim, cross-reference it against verified external documentation sources, and output a table showing: *Claimed State*, *True Verified State*, and the *Root Mathematical Pattern* that likely caused the error.

### 💼 Assignment 2: The Multi-Tier ROI Matrix Pitch (Module 6 / Lesson 6.3)
*   **Objective:** Apply the economics of AI to right-size real-world enterprise workflows.
*   **The Task:** Students take a massive, real-world case study of a corporation spending $15,000 monthly on premium customer service ticket summaries using top-tier frontier models.
*   **Deliverable:** Create an interactive spreadsheet and a 3-slide cost-benefit presentation. The student must map out a multi-tiered architecture that offloads 80% of repetitive token tasks to ultra-cheap localized models (like Llama or Haiku), reserves frontier models exclusively for high-stakes escalations, and maps out the explicit annual cost-savings vs. human labor overhead.

### 🛡️ Assignment 3: NIST AI Risk Mapping Drill (Module 5 / Lesson 5.1)
*   **Objective:** Map daily user prompting habits directly to standard federal compliance guardrails.
*   **The Task:** Analyze a simulated corporate security incident where a team member accidentally leaked sensitive financial projection metrics into a public web-facing model to clean up a spreadsheet.
*   **Deliverable:** Build an AI Risk Remediation Log following the **NIST AI RMF Core**. The student must detail: How the incident violates the *Govern* pillar, how to *Map* downstream liabilities regarding data weights, and how to configure a multi-stage system prompt that automatically screens out corporate identifiers before information passes through an API endpoint.
